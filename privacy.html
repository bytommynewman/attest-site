Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026. This policy covers the Attest app for Confluence Cloud ("the app").

Who we are

The app is operated by Tommy Newman ("we"). Contact: tommy_newman@icloud.com.

Where data lives

The app is built on Atlassian Forge and qualifies for Runs on Atlassian: it has no servers of its own and makes no network requests outside Atlassian. All data the app stores is held in Forge storage, within Atlassian's infrastructure, in the region Atlassian assigns to your site. We cannot access your site's data from outside the app.

What the app stores

DataWhyRetention
Per page: approval state, page version, owner account id, approval and review dates, and the list of signatures (account id, display name as configured, role, UTC timestamp, version)To run the approval workflow and render the signature recordWhile the app is installed, then as set by Atlassian's Forge data retention
Per space: the configured approvers (account id, display name, role), quorum, review period and locking preferenceTo apply the space's workflowWhile the app is installed, then as set by Atlassian's Forge data retention
Audit trail: for each state change, the acting account id and display name, the page, space, version, UTC timestamp and any note the actor typedTo provide an append-only record for review and auditWhile the app is installed, then as set by Atlassian's Forge data retention. The app provides no way to edit or delete individual entries, by design

Account ids and display names are personal data under GDPR and similar laws. They are processed on the instruction of the Confluence site administrator, who is the controller; we act as a processor through Atlassian's platform.

What the app reads but does not keep

Page version numbers, existing page restrictions (recorded only while a page is locked, then discarded), and whether the current user administers a space. The app never reads page content.

What the app writes to Confluence

Edit restrictions on approved pages, and page comments when an approval is reset or a review date passes.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. There are no analytics, tracking or advertising components.

Your rights

Requests to access, correct or erase personal data should go to your Confluence site administrator, who can act within the product. For anything else, contact tommy_newman@icloud.com.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when the policy changes.
