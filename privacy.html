Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026. This policy covers the Attest app for Confluence Cloud ("the app").

Who we are

The app is operated by Tommy Newman ("we"). Contact: tommy_newman@icloud.com.

Where data lives

The app is built on Atlassian Forge and designed to meet the Runs on Atlassian requirements: it has no servers of its own and makes no network requests outside Atlassian. All data the app stores is held in Forge storage, within Atlassian's infrastructure, in the region Atlassian assigns to your site. We have no route to your stored data from outside the app. Errors go to Forge's logs with ids, HTTP statuses and, in a few diagnostic cases, request details or short extracts of a Confluence error response, not page titles or bodies. We can see those logs only for sites that share them with us.

Nobody can edit or delete individual entries through the app, by design, apart from the closed-account removal described below

The app also keeps a register of which account ids appear on which pages, with each account's display name, and reports those account ids to Atlassian weekly, as Atlassian's privacy guidelines require. That report goes to Atlassian only.

When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian keeps its stored data for 28 days. It can be recovered if requested within 21 days, and is then deleted. Export your audit trail before uninstalling.

Account ids and display names are personal data under GDPR and similar laws. They are processed on the instruction of the Confluence site administrator, who is the controller; we act as a processor through Atlassian's platform.

What the app reads but does not keep

Page version numbers and who published them, view restrictions (sent back unchanged when edit restrictions are written), and whether the current user administers a space.

The app also reads a page's title and body on submit, on approve, and when an approved document is viewed. It computes a SHA-256 fingerprint from them and stores only that fingerprint. It doesn't store or send the title or body. A fingerprint can't be turned back into the text.

What the app writes to Confluence

Edit restrictions on approved pages, and page comments when a document is submitted or sent back, when an approval is reset, 14 days before a review date, and when a review date passes. Those comments mention the people concerned, so Confluence notifies them in the usual way.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. There are no analytics, tracking or advertising components.

Your rights

Requests to access, correct or erase personal data should go to your Confluence site administrator. When Atlassian reports an account closed, the app removes that person's account id and display name from its records, including the audit trail, within about a week. Those audit entries are marked redacted. The app offers no other way to change or delete individual audit entries, so contact us about any request it can't meet. For anything else, contact tommy_newman@icloud.com.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when the policy changes.
