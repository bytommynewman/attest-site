Pricing questions.

Who counts as a user?

Everyone in your Confluence site's user count, not only the approvers. A site with 25 users pays for 25.

Do I pay for more approvers or more documents?

No. The price depends only on the site's user count. Approvers, spaces and documents don't change it.

What happens at 11 users?

Once you pass 10, every user is counted from the first. A site with 11 users would pay $22 a month, which is 11 × $2.00.

Are any features held back at smaller sizes?

No. There are no feature tiers. A free site of 10 gets the same app as a site of 1,000.

Who bills me?

Atlassian, once the app is listed. There's nothing to pay before that.

What if we have more than 1,000 users?

Email us, and we'll agree a price with you before you install.

How do we get early access?

A site admin clicks Install on the early access page and approves it like any other app. It takes about two minutes.

Can we stop using it?

Yes, by uninstalling it at any time. Export your audit trail first: Atlassian keeps an uninstalled app's data for 28 days, and it can only be recovered if you ask within 21.
